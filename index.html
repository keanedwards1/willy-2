WAYS I WORK WITH PEOPLE

Curanderismo

Curanderismo is the traditional Mexican folk medicine that addresses the person’s physical, psychological & spiritual needs. The goal is sustaining health by creating a balance between the individual and their environment.

Guillermo offers private sessions at his East Bay Home.

Movement

As a Movement teacher for over 45 years (tai chi ch’uan, dance & various embodiment practices) Wm Guillermo offers sessions that provide an in-depth approach that identify one’s movement patterns, develop a greater sense of grounding, balance and alignment and cultivate an effective & fun daily practice.

Ministry

Wm Guillermo Ortiz is an ordained minister who received his Master of Divinity in 2006 from the Seminary of Spiritual Peacemaking in Ashland, Oregon.

He conducts weddings, coming-of-age initiations, last rites and public ceremonies.
